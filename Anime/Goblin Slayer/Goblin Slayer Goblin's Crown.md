> [!summary] Synopsis
> Due to his recent accomplishments, Goblin Slayer has become well known among adventurers. His name has now spread so far that one day, he receives a letter pleading for him to rescue a noble daughter who has gone missing during a mission involving goblins. Wasting no time, Goblin Slayer accepts the quest alongside his party members: Priestess, Elf, Dwarf Shaman, and Lizardman.
>
> Although the quest seems to be proceeding as it should, the party soon discovers a highly intelligent goblin paladin organizing the savage horde. Even with the enemy being far more formidable than initially expected, Goblin Slayer will offer no quarter to his hated foe, using any underhanded tactics and esoteric tools necessary to put the monster down.
