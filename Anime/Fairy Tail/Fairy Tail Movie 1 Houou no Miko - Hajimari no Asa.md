>[!summary] Synopsis
>Under the scorching desert sun, a lonely girl called Éclair wanders, faithfully protecting the relic "Phoenix Stone" entrusted to her. Even though she hates magic, the power of the Stone brings her handcrafted plush toy to life. Finally graced with the warmth of friendship, Éclair names it Momon, and together, they commence their journey across Fiore, guided by the Stone.
