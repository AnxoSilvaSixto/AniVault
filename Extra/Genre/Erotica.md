>[!info] Definition
>Erotica is a genre category used to classify adult titles that feature a high degree of sensual and sexually charged situations and themes, positioning it as a distinct classification between [[Ecchi]] and [[Hentai]].
>
>Unlike [[Ecchi]], which prioritizes sexual humor, Erotica titles often treat their themes of sexuality, lust, and nudity with a serious, sensual, or dramatic focus, intending to arouse or explore mature dynamics rather than purely generate comedy.
>
>However, similar to [[Ecchi]], and crucially differentiating it from [[Hentai]], Erotica stops short of depicting explicit sexual intercourse or exposed genitalia. The content remains within the realm of softcore adult entertainment, often utilizing visual implications, suggestive sounds, shadows, and the prelude or aftermath of sexual activity to convey its mature themes. Titles in this category are typically designated with a restrictive R+ (Mild Nudity) rating.
