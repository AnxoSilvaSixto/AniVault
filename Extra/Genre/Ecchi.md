>[!info] Definition
>Ecchi (derived from the first letter of the word Hentai, which is pronounced "e-chi" in Japanese, and means "naughty" or "sexy") is a genre of anime and manga characterized by lighthearted, non-explicit sexual humor and suggestive situations. These titles focus heavily on fan service designed primarily for comedic effect, frequently utilizing situations such as accidental groping, wardrobe malfunctions, revealing clothing, strategic camera angles, and sexual innuendo.
>
>Crucially, Ecchi content maintains a clear line by stopping short of depicting explicit sexual intercourse or genitalia, thereby distinguishing it from adult pornography. Its content is generally contained within non-explicit demographics, serving as an element of comedy or softcore visual interest.
