>[!info] Definition
>Hentai (literally "perversion" or "transformation") is the Japanese term used universally in the West to refer to explicitly pornographic anime, manga, and media. The genre's sole or primary intent is sexual gratification, making it distinct from all other categories.
>
>Hentai features graphic depictions of sexual acts, intercourse, and exposed genitalia, and is defined by its unambiguous, hardcore sexual content. Its content is produced exclusively for an adult audience, often featuring themes that explore various fetishes and highly mature subject matter, typically including visual censoring (such as pixelation or lens flares) applied to certain explicit elements to comply with local censorship laws.
