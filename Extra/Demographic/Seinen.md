>[!Info] Definition
>Seinen (literally "young man") is the demographic category aimed at young adult men, typically those between the ages of 18 and 40. Serving as a progression from [[Shounen]], Seinen content explores a wide and diverse range of genres, but with a significantly more mature, complex, and sometimes cynical approach. Narratives frequently delve into sophisticated themes involving [[Psychological|psychology]], politics, philosophy, and societal issues, often featuring protagonists with ambiguous morality or realistic, flawed character development. Seinen titles are known for their willingness to employ stark realism, graphic violence, or erotic elements, prioritizing nuanced storytelling and thematic depth over the simple, idealistic structures common in younger demographics.
