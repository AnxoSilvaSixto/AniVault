>[!info] Definition
>The Kids demographic, often referred to as Kodomomuke (子供向け, "directed at children"), targets the youngest audience, generally children under the age of 12. These series are crafted with simple, light-hearted, and colorful visual designs to be easily accessible to young viewers. The storytelling typically focuses on providing moral and educational value, emphasizing universally positive themes such as friendship, family bonds, teamwork, and ethical conduct. Content often revolves around bright [[adventure]], simple [[comedy]], and [[fantasy]], deliberately avoiding complex mature themes, graphic violence, or dark psychological elements to maintain an appropriate and wholesome viewing experience.
