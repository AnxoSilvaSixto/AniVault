>[!info] Definition
>Josei (literally "woman") is a demographic category primarily targeted toward adult women, typically spanning ages 18 to 40. These titles offer a mature and realistic depiction of life, distinguishing themselves from the often-idealized stories found in [[Shoujo]] works. Narratives frequently focus on the complexities of everyday adult experiences, including workplace challenges, professional careers, marital strife, motherhood, and family dynamics. Josei stories delve into deep emotional and psychological territory, exploring the nuances of mature romantic and interpersonal relationships with authenticity and sometimes painful honesty. While encompassing genres like [[Slice of Life]] and [[Drama]], the core appeal lies in grounded character development and relatable, often non-idealized, situations concerning personal growth and self-discovery.
