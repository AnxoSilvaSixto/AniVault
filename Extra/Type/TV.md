>[!info] Definition
>Anime produced specifically for broadcast on television networks, typically released as weekly episodes that run for one or more full seasons. This format is the standard release method for most mainstream series.
>
>It is distinct from formats such as [[Movie|Movies]] (theatrical release), [[OVA|OVAs]] (direct-to-video), and [[ONA|ONAs]] (online streaming release), which have different distribution models and typically shorter run times or non-linear episode structures.
