>[!info] Definition
>Anime adapted from Japanese young adult novels (ranobe) featuring illustrations.

>[!question] Context
>A primary driver of modern anime trends, particularly in the [[Fantasy]] and [[Isekai]] genres. Light Novels are typically shorter than standard literature, written with simplified prose, and feature [[Manga|manga-style]] illustrations interspersed throughout the text. Anime adaptations of this source often face the challenge of translating dense internal monologues and extensive world-building exposition into visual storytelling.
