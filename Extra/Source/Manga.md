>[!info] Definition
>The traditional standard for anime adaptations, sourced from Japanese comics and graphic novels.

>[!question] Context
>This refers to works originally serialized in print magazines (such as Shonen Jump or Afternoon). Manga sources provide a distinct visual storyboard for animators to follow. These adaptations can range from faithful panel-for-panel recreations to loose interpretations. This category covers all demographics ([[Shounen]], [[Seinen]], [[Shoujo]], [[Josei]]) and is distinguishable from [[Web manga|"Web manga"]] by its origin in traditional publishing houses.
