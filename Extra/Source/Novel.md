>[!info] Definition
>Anime adapted from general literature or mainstream fiction.

>[!question] Context
>Distinct from [[Light novel|Light Novels]], this source category refers to standard prose books ("bungei") aimed at the general public or adult demographics. These works usually lack illustrations and are published in standard book formats (tankōbon or bunkobon). Anime adapted from these sources tend to feature more grounded, literary, or [[Avant Garde|avant-garde]] storytelling and are less reliant on current [[Otaku culture|otaku subculture]] tropes.
