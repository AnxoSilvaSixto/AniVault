>[!info] Definition
>Anime adapted from four-cell comic strips (known as yonkoma). 

>[!question] Context
>Unlike traditional [[Manga|manga]] layouts, 4-koma utilizes a strict vertical structure of four panels per strip to tell a joke or brief story. Anime adaptations of this source material typically retain a [[Gag Humor|gag-based]] or [[Slice of life|slice-of-life]] nature. The narrative structure often follows kishōtenketsu (introduction, development, twist, and conclusion) within seconds. These adaptations may result in short-form anime (3–10 minute episodes) or stitch multiple strips together to form full-length episodes.
