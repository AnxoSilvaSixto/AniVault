>[!info] Definition
>Anime created directly for the screen without prior publication in other media.

>[!question] Context
>These works are conceptualized by the animation studio or a production committee from scratch. Because they are not bound by the pacing of a [[Manga|manga]] or the text of a [[Novel|novel]], Original anime are often praised for their cohesive pacing and visual-first direction. However, they carry higher financial risk as they lack a pre-existing fanbase to guarantee viewership.
