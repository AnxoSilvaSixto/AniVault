>[!info] Definition
>A miscellaneous category for anime derived from unconventional or mixed-media sources.

>[!question] Context
>This classification captures works that do not fit into standard publication formats. It includes anime adapted from picture books, stage plays, radio dramas, music videos, or multimedia franchises (such as idol projects or toy lines) where the anime is developed simultaneously with other merchandise rather than adapting a specific [[Manga|book]] or [[Game|game]].
