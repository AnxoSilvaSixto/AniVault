>[!info] Definition
>Anime developed as part of a coordinated project across multiple media, rather than being adapted from a single prior work.

>[!question] Context
>This category covers franchises conceived simultaneously as [[Manga|manga]], [[Game|games]], [[Novel|novels]], music projects, merchandise, or other related media, with the anime serving as one component of a broader transmedia strategy. Unlike a straightforward [[Original|original]] work or a single-source adaptation, mixed media projects are built around shared branding and interconnected release plans. These adaptations often require balancing narrative coherence with cross-promotional goals, and may draw on several source materials at once rather than one canonical medium.
