>[!info] Definition
>Anime adapted from interactive fiction games featuring static graphics and text-heavy narratives.

>[!question] Context
>Distinct from the [[Game|"Game"]] category, Visual Novels focus on reading and making choices rather than active gameplay mechanics. A major challenge in these adaptations is the "route" system; studios must decide whether to adapt a single romantic/story route, combine multiple routes into a hybrid story, or reset the timeline to show different outcomes (omnibus format).
