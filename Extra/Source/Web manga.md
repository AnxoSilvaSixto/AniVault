>[!info] Definition
>Anime adapted from comics published primarily via digital platforms.

>[!question] Context
>This source category has exploded in popularity with the advent of platforms like Pixiv, Twitter (X), and various Webtoon apps. Unlike traditional [[Manga|manga]], these works may utilize full color or vertical-scrolling layouts (Webtoons) optimized for smartphones. These series often start as independent, user-submitted content before gaining enough popularity to warrant an anime production.
