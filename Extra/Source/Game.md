>[!info] Definition
>Anime derived from video games involving active gameplay mechanics.

>[!question] Context
>This category encompasses adaptations of console games, arcade titles, MMORPGs, and mobile gacha games. Distinct from [[Visual novel|Visual Novels]], these sources rely on gameplay loops (combat, puzzles, strategy) which must be translated into a passive viewing experience. Writers often have to flesh out silent protagonists or condense hundreds of hours of grinding and side-quests into a linear narrative structure.
