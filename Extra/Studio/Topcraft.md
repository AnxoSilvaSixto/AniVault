>[!tip] Information
>Topcraft was a studio established by former [[Toei Animation]] staff. It's founder was animator Toru Hara. It is most famous for producing Hayao Miyazaki's film [[Kaze no Tani no Nausicaä|Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind]].
>
>In 1985 the studio filed for bankruptcy and it's staff were divided. Hayao Miyazaki, Toshio Suzuki and Isao Takahata acquired the studio itself and established [[Studio Ghibli]], while other staff left to form a new company called Pacific Animation Corporation.
