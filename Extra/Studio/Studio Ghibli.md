>[!tip] Information
>Studio Ghibli (Studio Ghibli, Inc.) is a Japanese animation studio based in Koganei, Tokyo. It is best known for its range of animated feature films, and has also produced several short subjects, television commercials, and two television films. Its mascot and most recognizable symbol is a character named Totoro, a giant spirit inspired by raccoon dogs (Tanuki) and cats from the 1988 anime film [[Tonari no Totoro|My Neighbor Totoro]]. Among the studio's highest-grossing films are [[Sen to Chihiro no Kamikakushi|Spirited Away]] (2001), [[Howl no Ugoku Shiro|Howl's Moving Castle]] (2004) and [[Gake no Ue no Ponyo|Ponyo]] (2008). The studio was founded on June 15, 1985, by the directors Hayao Miyazaki and Isao Takahata and producer Toshio Suzuki, after acquiring [[Topcraft]]'s assets.
