>[!tip] Information
>White Fox is an animation studio founded April 2007 by former members of [[OLM|OLM's]] Team Iwasa.
>
>Its founder and current representative director is Iwasa Gaku and its office resides in Tokyo, Suginami.
>
>The studio was acquired by publisher AlphaPolis on September 30, 2025.
