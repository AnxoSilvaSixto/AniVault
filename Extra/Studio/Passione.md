> [!tip] Information
> Passione is a Japanese animation studio founded by Kazuhiro Saitō, a former production manager at Studio Fantasia. The company is headquartered in Asagayaminami, Suginami, Tokyo, Japan. In 2023, Passione, alongside Hayabusa Film and Saetta, opened an anime-themed café and bar named Anime Cafe&Bar CUE LAMP in Osaka. Passione has produced notable series such as High School DxD Hero, Citrus, Mieruko-chan, and Watashi no Yuri wa Oshigoto Desu!.
