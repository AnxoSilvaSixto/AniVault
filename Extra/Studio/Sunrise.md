>[!tip] Information
>Sunrise (SUNRISE Inc., Japanese: サンライズ) is a Japanese animation studio and production brand of Bandai Namco Filmworks. Founded by former Mushi Production members in September 1972 as Sunrise Studio, renamed Nippon Sunrise in November 1976, operating as Sunrise since 1987, merged in April 2022 with the film business of Bandai Namco Arts to form Bandai Namco Filmworks while keeping Sunrise as a brand.
>
>Among Japan's largest studios, Sunrise is renowned for original series such as [[Cowboy Bebop]], [[Cowboy Bebop Tengoku no Tobira|Cowboy Bebop: Tengoku no Tobira]] and [[Code Geass Hangyaku no Lelouch|Code Geass: Hangyaku no Lelouch]], plus many mecha titles. Former Sunrise producer Masahiko Minami later founded [[Bones]].
