>[!tip] Information
>Xebec was originally a subsidiary of the anime studio [[Production I.G]] and it specialised in the production of television anime. Xebec became independent in 2007.
>
>After a long period of financial struggles Xebec ceased to exist as a business entity on May 31, 2019.
>Xebec's assets have been divvied up among several companies: the animation production and production management divisions have been sold to [[Sunrise]], which utilized these resources to create a new company. The studio in Nerima Ward in Tokyo (formerly Xebec Zwei, now I.G Zwei) continued working on the Fafner in the Azure - THE BEYOND series. The animation coloring and finishing division were transferred to [[Signal.MD]].
