>[!tip] Information
>Studio Deen is an animation production company.
>
>Founded in 1975 by Hasegawa Hiroshi, an [[Sunrise|ex-Sunrise Studio]] staffer. Initially it was a finishing/cell retouch studio as Hasegawa himself worked on finishing/clean-up inspection at [[Sunrise]]. In 1982 Deen became an animation production company.
