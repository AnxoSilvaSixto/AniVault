>[!tip] Information
>Satelight is an animation studio, founded on December 1995 in Sapporo, Hokkaido, best known for creating the anime series Arjuna and Geneshaft.
>
>The name Satelight consists of S for Sapporo, A for Animate, T for Technology and E for Entertainment.
