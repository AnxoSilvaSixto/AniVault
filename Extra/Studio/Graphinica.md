>[!tip] Information
>Graphinica is a photography, CG, VFX and animation studio based in Tokyo. On 22.04.2009 Q-tec bought Gonzo's digital department (62 people) that became Graphinica, the company started activities the next month.
>
>In 2010 Q-tec transferred Decoloco (another Q-tec subsidiary) staff to Graphinica. In 2011 an animation department was established.
