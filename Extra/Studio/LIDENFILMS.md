>[!tip] Information
>LIDENFILMS is a Japanese animation studio founded by Hiroaki Matsuura, Tetsurō Satomi, and Tadao Iwaki. The company is headquartered in Kamiogi, Suginami, Tokyo, Japan. LIDENFILMS one of the founding member of Ultra Super Pictures, a joint holding company that includes studios like Sanzigen, [[Trigger]], and Ordet. The studio has multiple branches across Japan, including Tokyo 1st and 2nd Studios, Kyoto Studio, Osaka Studio, and Fukuya Studio. They are known for producing series including Tokyo Revengers, Rurouni Kenshin (2023), [[Yofukashi no Uta|Call of the Night]], The Heroic Legend of Arslan, [[Yamada-kun to 7-nin no Majo|Yamada-kun and the Seven Witches]].
