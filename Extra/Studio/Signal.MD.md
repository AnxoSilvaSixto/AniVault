>[!tip] Information
>Signal.MD is an animation production studio and subsidiary of IG Port. It was announced that the studio was to be merged with by [[Production I.G]], effective June 1, 2025 with the aim to optimize resource allocation and enhance profitability across the IG Port group.
>
>Following Signal.MD's merger into [[Production I.G]], it ceased as a subsidiary company with a legal entity under IG Port; however, the name "Signal.MD" is still used by the same production team as a brand name.
