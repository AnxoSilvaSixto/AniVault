>[!tip] Information
>Kinema Citrus is a Japanese animation studio founded by former [[Production I.G]] and [[Bones]] staff. The company is based in Suginami, Tokyo, Japan. In 2019, Kadokawa and Bushiroad each acquired a 31.8% stake in the company, forming a strategic partnership to produce anime IPs. They are known for producing notable series such as [[Made in Abyss]],[[Tate no Yuusha no Nariagari|The Rising of the Shield Hero]], Barakamon, and .hack//Quantum.
