>[!tip] Information
>Brain's Base, Inc. is a Japanese animation studio founded in 1996 by former [[Tokyo Movie Shinsha]] staff. The studio is based in Tokyo, Japan. It is known for producing series such as [[Baccano!]], Durarara!!, Natsume Yuujinchou, and Oregairu.
